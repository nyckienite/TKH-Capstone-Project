The Knowledge House • Data Job Market Dashboard

Guided story of how data science & data analyst roles show up across skills, education, industry, geography, and company characteristics — aligned to The Knowledge House curriculum.

How to read this dashboard

  1. Start here: Use the global filters on the left to narrow by region, level, or work setup.
  2. Skills: Jump to “Skills Demand” to see which tools and languages show up most.
  3. Education: Visit “Education & Training” to compare postings vs. what programs teach.
  4. Industry: Use “Industry Gaps” to see where expectations and preparation don’t match.
  5. Roles: Check “Roles & Seniority” to compare junior vs. senior role expectations.
  6. Company & Skills: End with “Company & Skills” to see how company size + skill breadth connect.
